Our study analyses a newly released incident-level crime
dataset for the City of Cambridge, Massachusetts, which was
published through the Cambridge Police Department’s Annual
Crime Reports and open data portal in 2026-Apr. The
dataset spans reported crime from 2009 to 2026-Apr and contains
106,350 individual incidents. Each record corresponds to a
single reported event and includes a combination of administrative attributes and free-text offence descriptions. For each
incident, we use the following fields: 

(i) a unique incident identifier (File Number), 
(ii) the date and time of occurrence (Crime Date Time), 
(iii) a free-text offense description (Crime), 
(iv) a locally defined neighborhood and reporting area, and 
(v) spatial information provided as reporting area centroids. 

These fields support offence standardisation, spatial
aggregation, and temporal analysis in subsequent sections
